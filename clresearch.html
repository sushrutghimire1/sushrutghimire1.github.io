AI Research Engineer — 1 Year Operating System

This is not a generic course list. This roadmap is designed to transform you into a highly credible AI Research Engineer with a strong portfolio, systems depth, research capability, and visible public work. The goal at the end of 12 months is:

Technical Depth

PyTorch, transformers, probabilistic ML, distributed systems, MLOps, scientific computing.

Research Credibility

Paper reproductions, technical writing, experiments, open-source contributions, research intuition.

Public Reputation

Strong GitHub, polished flagship project, blogs, demos, technical communication.

Daily Non-Negotiables

  • 2–4 hours deep technical work
  • Read 1 research paper section daily
  • Write implementation notes
  • Code every single day
  • Exercise / physical movement
  • Maintain GitHub contribution streak
  • Track weekly learning reflections
  • 1 hour focused mathematics practice
